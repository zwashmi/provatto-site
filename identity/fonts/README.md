# Provatto — Fonts

The Provatto identity uses the **IBM Plex** family (SIL Open Font License 1.1 — free for commercial use, embedding, and web).

| Role | Family | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic (primary, RTL) | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** | 300 · 400 · 500 · 600 · 700 |
| Latin (pairing, wordmark, labels) | **IBM Plex Sans** | 400 · 500 · 600 · 700 |
| Numerals / serials / prices / dates | **IBM Plex Sans** (Latin digits, always) | 500 · 600 |

## Rules
- Arabic **always leads**; Latin supports. Never letterspace Arabic.
- Numbers, reference numbers, serials, SAR prices, and dates render in **Latin digits** with IBM Plex Sans — even inside Arabic text.
- Latin micro-labels: uppercase, ~0.18em tracking, usually gold.

## Where to get them
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic & IBM Plex Sans: https://github.com/IBM/plex (OFL-1.1) or Google Fonts.
- License files ship with the font download; keep `OFL.txt` alongside any embedded/self-hosted copies.

## DGA note
The original brief specified the Saudi **DGA** font for Arabic. It is licensed primarily for government platforms and needs **commercial-license verification** before use. Until confirmed, IBM Plex Sans Arabic is the approved Arabic face. To swap later, replace the font-face declarations only — the type scale and rules are unchanged.

*License-restricted font binaries are intentionally NOT bundled here. Download IBM Plex under OFL-1.1 and self-host, or load from Google Fonts.*
